# The Bell That Broke

A town that stopped ringing its bell must decide whether the silence was mercy or cowardice.

## S01 — The frozen square (8s)

**Camera:** panLeft — scan the square with the walk, keep the bell in frame

> the winter the river stopped, the bell stopped with it

*establishing: two figures cross the square toward the bell, camera pans with them*

## S02 — The keeper's threshold (7s)

**Camera:** dollyIn — close the distance to the keeper: pressure, not movement

> nine days, the keeper said, and nobody rings it but me

*threshold shot: warm interior against cold snow, the keeper blocks the door*

## S03 — Close-up plate (6s)

**Camera:** pushInSlow — hold on the face, let the realisation land

> he had rung it once before. it did not end well.

*realisation: push-in on the keeper's face, no camera trick beyond the move*

## S04 — The graveyard at first light (9s)

**Camera:** panRight — follow the digging, reveal the grave

> so the apprentice dug, and the ground rang instead

*the action beat: real digging motion, sun behind, long shadows*

## S05 — The bell (6s)

**Camera:** tiltUp — reveal the bell from rim to crown

> and the bell, at last, answered.

*insert: the object that resolves the story, dust in the light, no zoom-as-animation*
