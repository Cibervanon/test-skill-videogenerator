## Bugs fixed

- B-09 **critical**: the mix graph dropped narration; the delivered MP4 had score
  only. The mixer is deterministic NumPy, `assemble` prefers the mix, QA measures
  voice in the delivery and `voicecheck` rejects a mix without the stem.
- B-05 **high**: concurrent renders raced on the global active manifest. A
  fail-fast file lock now covers the whole command.
- B-01 mix sample rate (high, false audio verdict).
- B-02 QA crash on a manifest without `line` (medium).
- B-03 untimed burned-in captions (high quality; now chunk-timed to the take
  clock, 0 ms error against the plan).
- B-07 QA threshold naming (`loudness_band_db` with a backwards-compatible alias).
